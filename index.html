Projects at ARCADE Lab

Selected work from ARCADE Lab, mentored by Prof. Mathias Unberath and Dr. Roger D. Soberanis-Mukul

Real-Time Endoscopic 3D Reconstruction

ARCADE Lab ROS2 3D Slicer SLAM
  • Implemented real-time 3D reconstruction for sinus endoscopy using ROS2, Python, and computer vision.
  • Improved reconstruction framerate by 300%, enabling real-time clinical visualization workflows.
  • Registered camera trajectory into 3D Slicer with 3.2 mm / 4.9 degree pose accuracy across 10 cadaveric trials.

Camera Relocalization for Surgical Navigation

ARCADE Lab DINOv2 ORB / PnP Python
  • Built a training-free camera relocalization pipeline combining DINOv2-based global image retrieval with local feature matching and PnP pose recovery.
  • Reidentified the surgical region of interest and relocalized up to 78% of query frames without any fine-tuning or CT-space exhaustive matching.
  • Outperformed a prior global-matching baseline, cutting translation error to 1.9 mm and rotation error to 0.17 rad while running at real-time frame rates.

3D Mesh Reconstruction via Stereoscopic Imaging

ARPA-H / ARCADE Lab OneSLAM Stereo Depth Open3D
  • Developed a mosaicking pipeline that fuses monocular SLAM trajectories (OneSLAM) with stereoscopic depth estimation into a scaled, registered 3D mesh.
  • Designed a continuous scaling and outlier-rejection scheme to correct depth-derived scale drift, aligning reconstructed meshes to DVRK/ECM ground-truth geometry.
